# Practical Skills

Practical AI workflows with illustrated examples and complete prompts to copy.

## Commitment and Capacity

[Download the Word prompt](https://github.com/scottwojo17-bit/Practical-Skills/raw/refs/heads/main/commitment-capacity/Commitment_Capacity_Prompt.docx)

Open the document, copy the prompt, and paste it into a new chat. It will ask about your commitments and priorities, identify tradeoffs, and recommend a practical next step.

[View the infographic](commitment-capacity/Commitment_Capacity_Infographic.png)

[Read and copy the prompt](commitment-capacity/Prompt.md)

The infographic uses an illustrative scenario. The prompt does not install a skill, change your calendar, or take actions for you.
